Santa Riddle – Day 14

Can you help Santa remember the punchlines?

It is the 14th of December and the North Pole is buzzing with excitement. Santa has prepared gifts, packed his sleigh and practised his “Ho Ho Ho!” – but there is one more important job: his famous Christmas joke show for the elves.

When Santa looks at his list of jokes, he gasps. “Oh dear… I mixed up all the questions and answers!” he sighs. “If I tell the wrong punchline, the reindeer will laugh at me for weeks!”

Santa needs a clever helper who can match each question with the correct funny answer. Are you ready to connect them again so the Christmas show can go on?
